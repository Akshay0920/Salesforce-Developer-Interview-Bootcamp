# Reflection

## What Went Well?

Successfully updated the Application status and verified the changes in Salesforce.

## What Was Difficult?

Understanding how to update an existing record instead of creating a new one.

## What Would I Improve?

Separate status update logic from other business operations if additional update scenarios are introduced in the future.

## Engineering Lesson

Updating existing business data should be done carefully to preserve data integrity and accurately reflect changes in the business process.
