# Reflection

## What Went Well?

Successfully integrated all previous sprint functionality into a complete application workflow.

## What Was Difficult?

Testing every business scenario and verifying that each validation executed in the correct order.

## What Would I Improve?

Separate duplicate checking, eligibility validation, and record creation into helper methods for better readability and maintainability.

## Engineering Lesson

A complete business process should validate data before performing database operations and should always return meaningful messages to the user.
