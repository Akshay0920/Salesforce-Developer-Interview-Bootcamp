# Reflection

## What Went Well?

Successfully integrated all engineering sprint features into one complete business transaction.

## What Was Difficult?

Testing multiple scenarios and ensuring each validation executed before database changes.

## What Would I Improve?

Further modularize the service by separating validation and DML responsibilities into additional helper methods as the project grows.

## Engineering Lesson

A well-designed service retrieves only the required information, validates all business rules, and updates data only after every validation has passed.
